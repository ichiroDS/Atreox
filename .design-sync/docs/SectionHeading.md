The editorial serif section headline — Playfair Display 500 with responsive `clamp()` sizing, white, tight leading. Every major section title on the site uses this.

```jsx
<SectionHeading>Why ATREOX is different</SectionHeading>
<SectionHeading style={{ marginBottom: 22 }}>What ATREOX does</SectionHeading>
```

Typically preceded by a `SectionBadge` overline (16–18px gap) and followed by body copy in Barlow 300.
